Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how FalseQuote collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

📋

1. Information We Collect (and Do Not Collect)

We believe in data minimization. Here is exactly what we collect and what we do not collect:

  • Form Data (NOT COLLECTED): Any information you fill into the quotation or invoice forms (client names, item details, prices, etc.) is never sent to our servers. This data stays entirely on your device (in browser local storage).
  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business details provided during account creation (if applicable).
  • Payment Information: Transaction metadata processed securely through Razorpay (like payment IDs). We do not store your card or UPI credentials.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and basic session information collected via analytics.
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and IP address for security and analytics purposes.
⚙️

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for providing and improving our services:

  • To generate, preview, and download professional quotations and invoices.
  • To process payments securely through Razorpay payment gateway.
  • To send you transaction confirmations and service-related communications.
  • To improve our platform features and user experience.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure platform security.
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations.
💳

3. Payment & Financial Data

FalseQuote uses Razorpay as its payment gateway for processing all payments. PDF download pricing starts at ₹30 for 1–2 pages and increases by ₹10 for each additional page.

  • All payment transactions are processed by Razorpay. We do not store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Payment data is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.
  • Razorpay's privacy policy applies to all payment transactions: razorpay.com/privacy
  • Our registered UPI ID for receiving payments is: nk6818237@oksbi
🗄️

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your quotation and invoice data is completely private. It is stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). We do not transfer this data to our servers.

  • Browser local storage data remains on your device until you clear it manually.
  • Payment transaction records (metadata only, not your PDF content) are retained for up to 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations.
  • Analytics data (anonymous usage) is retained for up to 30 days.
  • You can delete your browser data at any time by clearing local storage in your browser settings.
🤝

5. Sharing of Information

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Razorpay: Payment processing data is shared with Razorpay as necessary to complete transactions.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government authority in India.
  • Business Transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred under the same privacy protections.
🍪

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

FalseQuote uses browser-based storage mechanisms rather than traditional cookies:

  • localStorage: Used to store your quotation data, visitor ID, and analytics events locally on your device.
  • sessionStorage: Used to store temporary session information during your current browser session.
  • We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies.
  • You can clear all stored data by clearing your browser's site data at any time.
🔒

7. Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:

  • All data transmission between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Payment signature verification is performed using HMAC-SHA256 cryptography via Razorpay.
  • Admin access to payment records is protected by a secure API key.
  • We regularly review our security practices to protect against unauthorized access.
✅

8. Your Rights

As a user of FalseQuote, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com

👶

9. Children's Privacy

FalseQuote is a professional business tool not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately at supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com.
