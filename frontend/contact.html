❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to download a PDF?

FalseQuote charges ₹30 for 1–2 PDF pages, then adds ₹10 for each page after the second. This applies to both Quotations and Invoices. Payment is processed through Razorpay.

I paid but didn't receive the PDF. What should I do?

Please email us at supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com with your Razorpay Payment ID (starts with "pay_"). We'll verify and either generate your PDF manually or issue a full refund within 5–7 working days.

How do I request a refund?

Email us at supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com with subject "Refund Request – [Your Payment ID]" within 7 days of the transaction. Include your name, payment ID, and reason. We'll respond within 24–48 hours. For full details, see our Refund Policy.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway. FalseQuote never stores your card details, UPI PIN, or any payment credentials. All transactions are encrypted with SSL/TLS.

Can I edit my quotation after generating the PDF?

Yes! FalseQuote stores your quotation data in your browser's local storage. You can edit and regenerate documents. Each new PDF download is priced by page count: ₹30 for 1–2 pages, plus ₹10 for every page after the second.

What if I accidentally closed the page after paying?

Your data is saved in your browser's local storage. Reopen the quotation/invoice tool and your data should be intact. If you paid but closed before downloading, contact us with your Payment ID and we'll assist you.

Is my business data stored on your servers?

No. Your quotation and invoice data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (localStorage). It does not get uploaded to our servers. Only payment transaction records (not your document content) are stored on our secure database for compliance purposes.

How do I contact FalseQuote support?

Email us at supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com. We respond within 24–48 working hours (Monday–Saturday).
