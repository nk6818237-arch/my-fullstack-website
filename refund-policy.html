Refund & Cancellation Policy

We strive to provide the best service. This policy explains when and how refunds are processed for FalseQuote payments.

📖

1. Overview

FalseQuote charges ₹30 for 1–2 PDF pages, with ₹10 added for each page after the second, for both Quotations and Invoices. Since we provide a digital service, our refund policy is designed to be fair to both parties.

All payments are processed through Razorpay. This policy is in compliance with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and guidelines applicable to digital/online services in India.

📊

2. Refund Eligibility Summary

Scenario Refund Status Timeframe
PDF successfully downloaded Not Eligible —
Payment deducted but PDF not generated (our error) Full Refund 5–7 working days
Duplicate payment charged Full Refund 5–7 working days
Wrong amount charged Full Refund 5–7 working days
Changed mind after payment Not Eligible —
Bank error or gateway failure Case-by-Case 7–10 working days
✅

3. When You Are Eligible for a Refund

A refund will be issued in the following situations:

  • Technical Failure: Payment was deducted from your account, but the PDF was not generated due to a server or technical error on our side.
  • Double Charge: Your account was charged more than once for the same document download.
  • Wrong Amount: You were charged an amount different from the page-based price shown before payment.
  • Gateway Error: A verified issue with the Razorpay gateway caused an incorrect charge.
❌

4. When Refunds Are NOT Applicable

Refunds will not be provided in the following situations:

  • The PDF was successfully generated and downloaded to your device.
  • You changed your mind or no longer need the document after downloading.
  • The document content was incorrect due to data entered by you (user error).
  • The refund request was made more than 7 days after the transaction date.
  • Payment disputes initiated without first contacting our support team.
📩

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  • Send an email to supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com within 7 days of the transaction.
  • Use the subject line: "Refund Request – [Your Razorpay Payment ID]"
  • Include: Your name, Razorpay Payment ID (starts with "pay_"), transaction date, and reason for refund.
  • Attach a screenshot of the payment confirmation if available.

We will acknowledge your request within 24–48 working hours and process eligible refunds within 5–7 working days.

🏦

6. Refund Processing

All approved refunds are processed through Razorpay to your original payment method:

  • Credit/Debit Card: Refund credited to the same card within 5–7 working days.
  • UPI: Refund credited to the same UPI-linked bank account within 3–5 working days.
  • Net Banking: Refund credited within 5–7 working days.

Refund timelines depend on your bank's processing speed. FalseQuote is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment networks.

🚫

7. Cancellation Policy

Since FalseQuote provides an instant digital service (PDF generation upon payment), cancellation of an order is not possible once payment is initiated.

  • If payment is in progress (pending status), you may close the payment window, and no charge will be made.
  • Once Razorpay confirms the payment as successful, the transaction cannot be cancelled.
  • In case of a pending transaction that is debited but not confirmed, it will auto-reverse within 5–7 working days by your bank.
⚖️

8. Dispute Resolution

If you are dissatisfied with our refund decision, you may:

  • Email us again with additional evidence at supporthelpfalsequote@gmail.com
  • Contact Razorpay's customer support for payment-related disputes.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Consumer Forum under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (for amounts above ₹1 lakh).

We are committed to resolving all disputes fairly and transparently.
